Planning note 01

Choose the feeling
before the flowers.

Begin with three words you want guests to use after the celebration. Intimate, joyful, ceremonial. Or vibrant, generous, unexpected. Let those words guide decisions when too many options begin to look equally beautiful.

Planning note 02

Give every ritual
its own breathing room.

Speak with your families and officiant about timing, customs and participation before shaping the wider schedule. A beautiful programme respects the moments that should never feel hurried.

Planning note 03

Think about the arrival.
Then the farewell.

How will guests find their way? Where can older relatives sit comfortably? What will people hear as they enter? Consider the whole journey alongside the visual design. Accessibility and comfort belong in the first conversation.
